# SIN - School of AI | Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity & Overview
- **Brand Name**: SIN - School of AI
- **Flagship Program**: SIN AI Agent Xcelerator Program 2026
- **Tagline**: *Don't Prepare Students Only to Use the Future. Train Them to Build It.*
- **Persona**: Elite, authoritative, academic yet intensely practical, futuristic, and career-accelerating.
- **Logo Asset**: `brand/logo.png` (Heraldic Lions Rampant with modern bold SIN typography on Imperial Crimson background).

## 3. Typography & Aesthetics
- **Primary Font**: `Inter`, `Outfit`, `-apple-system`, sans-serif
- **Styling**: Sleek dark mode with Imperial Crimson atmospheric glow, glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur`), rounded corners (`12px` to `16px`), sharp clean borders.
- **Tone of Voice**:
  - Direct, encouraging, ambitious.
  - Highlights real building (70% practical) over passive theoretical consumption.
  - Emphasizes elite exclusivity (max 25 students per cohort).
